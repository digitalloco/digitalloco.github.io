digitalloco is a personal hobby project by Phil Claridge: hardware
and firmware for digital model railways, starting with a DCC command
station and track detectors. It is currently a hobby project, and
nothing here is for sale.

## Who

Software architect emeritus -- retired, so the boxes and arrows are
now drawn for fun rather than for a living. Chief drawer of boxes and
arrows. System whisperer. Also, when needed, blueprint-and-soldering-iron
man.

Model railway electronics is a busman's holiday: firmware, protocols
and an oscilloscope on the bench, the same tools as the day job, only
now the customer is a model locomotive.

Email [phil@digitalloco.com](mailto:phil@digitalloco.com). More on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/claridge).

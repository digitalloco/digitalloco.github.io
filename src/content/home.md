Hardware and firmware for digital model railways: a DCC command
station and track detectors. Work in progress.

The documentation starts with an
[introduction to RailCom](/docs/railcom/introduction/), the DCC
feedback channel, and a guide to the
[RailCom standards](/docs/railcom/standards/) and which to read. A
list of [decoders and their RailCom features](/docs/railcom/decoders/)
is under construction.
